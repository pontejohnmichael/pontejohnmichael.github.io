AI Inquiry Desk for a Contractor

  • Problem: project inquiries from Instagram get lost or answered late.
  • Built: a Google Form feeds n8n; 3 AI agents score each inquiry, draft a personal reply and write a shift briefing.
  • Result: one click per shift turns every new inquiry into a prioritized, ready-to-send reply in a shared spreadsheet.

Real Estate Speed-to-Lead

  • Problem: agents lose leads that wait hours for a reply.
  • Built: a lead form and webhook into an AI analyst that scores hot, warm or cold, filters spam and writes a call script; a second agent replies in the lead's own language.
  • Result: every lead is logged to the CRM sheet and answered within a minute; hot leads trigger a "call now" alert.

Multi-Agent Lead Hunter
